Animated Power Circuit

Terminals 1, 2 — DC-bus port (V₁), C₁ across it Terminals 3, 4 — Battery / output port (V₂), C₂ across it S₁, S₂ — IGBTs, gate-driven, collector "a" / emitter "k" D₁, D₂ — antiparallel freewheeling diodes (cathode "−" toward the switch's collector side) L — single energy-storage inductor shared by both directions

Phase 1 — S₁ ON

The upper switch S₁ (collector "a" at terminal 1, emitter "k" at the switching node) is ON. The switching node is connected to the positive DC bus (terminal 1+). The inductor receives:
VL = V₁ − V₂
Therefore the inductor current rises.

Phase 2 — S₂ ON

S₁ is OFF and S₂'s antiparallel diode D₂ provides the freewheeling path (D₂ conducts emitter-to-collector, i.e. from terminal 2− up to the switching node). The inductor continues supplying energy to the battery/output port (terminals 3, 4).
VL = −V₂
The inductor current decreases.

Operating Modes

Mode Direction S₁ S₂ Inductor Action Power Flow
Mode 1 Forward ON OFF Current rises V₁ → V₂
Mode 2 Forward OFF ON Current falls V₁ → V₂
Mode 3 Reverse OFF ON Energy stored in L V₂ → L
Mode 4 Reverse ON OFF Energy delivered to bus L → V₁

Forward Buck Mode

V₂ = D V₁
D = V₂/V₁

Power flows from the DC bus to the battery/load.

Reverse Boost Mode

V₁ = V₂/(1−D)
D = 1 − V₂/V₁

Power flows from the battery/load toward the DC bus.

Dead Time

A short interval is inserted between turning OFF one switch and turning ON the complementary switch.

S₁ = OFF    →    DEAD TIME    →    S₂ = ON

Dead time prevents shoot-through, where both switches conduct simultaneously and effectively short the DC-link capacitor.

Numerical Problems

Problem 1 — Buck Duty Ratio

A bidirectional converter has a DC bus voltage of 100 V. The required battery voltage is 48 V. Determine the ideal duty ratio in forward buck operation.

D = V₂/V₁

D = 48/100 = 0.48

Duty ratio = 48%

Problem 2 — Buck Output Voltage

A converter operates in buck mode from a 200 V DC bus. If D = 0.35, determine the output voltage.

V₂ = D V₁

V₂ = 0.35 × 200

V₂ = 70 V

Problem 3 — Reverse Boost Duty Ratio

A battery voltage is 48 V and the DC-link voltage is 100 V. Determine the duty ratio required for ideal reverse boost operation.

D = 1 − V₂/V₁

D = 1 − 48/100

D = 0.52 = 52%

Problem 4 — Inductor Energy

An inductor of 100 μH carries 20 A. Calculate the energy stored.

EL = ½ L iL²

E = 0.5 × 100×10⁻⁶ × 20²

E = 0.02 J = 20 mJ

Problem 5 — Inductor Ripple Current

A buck converter has V₁ = 100 V, V₂ = 50 V, L = 100 μH and switching frequency of 20 kHz. Find the peak-to-peak inductor current ripple.

D = V₂/V₁ = 0.5
ΔiL = (V₁−V₂)D/(Lfs)

ΔiL = (100−50)(0.5) / (100×10⁻⁶×20000)

ΔiL = 12.5 A peak-to-peak

Problem 6 — Battery Current

A 48 V battery delivers 480 W. Determine the battery current assuming 100% efficiency.

I = P/V

I = 480/48

I = 10 A

Problem 7 — Converter Efficiency

A 48 V battery transfers 1 kW to a 100 V DC bus. The converter efficiency is 92%. Determine the battery current.

Pin = Pout/η

Pin = 1000/0.92

Pin = 1086.96 W

Ibattery = Pin/Vbattery
I = 22.65 A
