1. Design of Buck Converter

A buck converter is a DC-DC converter used to reduce a higher DC voltage to a lower regulated DC voltage. It is widely used for sensors, controllers, motor drivers, actuators and AI processor power supplies in robots.

Sensor Supply

48 V → 5 V

Low-voltage supply for sensors and measurement circuits.

Controller Board

48 V → 12 V

Power supply for microcontrollers and control electronics.

AI Processor

48 V → 12 V / 5 V

Provides regulated supply to embedded AI computing systems.

Motor Driver

48 V → 24 V

Auxiliary power for gate drivers and motor-control electronics.

2. Buck-Boost Converter for Drones, Humanoids and AGVs

A buck-boost converter is useful when the battery voltage can be either higher or lower than the required regulated voltage.

Drone

Battery voltage changes considerably during discharge. A buck-boost stage can maintain a regulated supply for electronics.

Humanoid Robot

The battery voltage varies while servo motors create rapidly changing loads.

AGV

A wide battery voltage range can be converted to a stable DC-link or auxiliary supply.

3. Isolated DC-DC Converter for ESC and Industrial Robots

An isolated DC-DC converter transfers electrical energy while providing galvanic isolation between input and output. A high-frequency transformer is generally used for energy transfer.

ESC Power Supply

An isolated supply can provide a protected low-voltage control/gate-driver supply for an electronic speed controller.

Industrial Robot

Isolation can separate high-power motor circuits from sensitive control and communication electronics.

Safety

Galvanic isolation prevents a direct conductive path between the two electrical domains.

4. Bidirectional DC-DC Converter

A bidirectional DC-DC converter allows power to flow in both directions between the battery and DC bus. This is particularly important in robotic systems using regenerative braking.

Operating Modes

Mode 1 – Motoring

Battery energy flows toward the DC bus and motor drive.

Battery → DC Bus → Motor

Mode 2 – Regenerative Braking

Motor kinetic energy is converted back into electrical energy.

Motor → DC Bus → Battery

Mode 3 – Battery Charging

The converter regulates current flowing from the DC bus toward the battery.

Mode 4 – Boost

Battery voltage can be boosted to the required DC bus voltage.

Why Bidirectional Conversion is Important in Robotics

  • Regenerative braking
  • Battery charging
  • DC-bus voltage regulation
  • Energy recovery
  • Improved battery utilization
  • Four-quadrant motor operation support
