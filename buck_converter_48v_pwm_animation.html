Phase 1 — Switch ON

Vx snaps up to Vin (48V). The voltage across the inductor is (Vin − Vout), which is positive, so inductor current ramps up. Energy flows from the source into L and the load.

Phase 2 — Switch OFF

Vx snaps down to ~0V (the diode clamps it, freewheeling). The inductor now sees −Vout across it, so current ramps down. It still supplies the load, just recirculating through the diode instead of the source.
